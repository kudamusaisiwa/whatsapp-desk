Privacy by design

No hosted inbox. No hidden key.

Desk AI for WhatsApp Web stores your business library, notes and OpenAI key in your local Chrome profile. When you ask for a draft, selected visible conversation content and images go directly to OpenAI using your key. Optional voice dictation sends a recording to OpenAI for transcription only after you click the microphone and grant access; the extension does not save the recording. Dictated text is editable and only enters the WhatsApp composer when you choose Send message; you press WhatsApp's Send button yourself. Optional call recording captures the call tab's audio together with your microphone only after you choose it in Chrome's sharing picker; the audio is sent to OpenAI for transcription, and for the optional call summary the transcript, your business name and any participant or speaker names are sent to OpenAI. The extension has no advertising analytics or general-purpose data backend.
