Privacy policy · effective 25 October 2026

Clear about your data.

WhatsApp Desk is an independent open-source project by Kudakwashe Musasiwa. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by Meta or WhatsApp.

What the extension handles

When you enable the relevant features, the extension handles visible WhatsApp Web conversations and contact details, your business documents and notes, loaded images or payment proofs, follow-ups, settings and the OpenAI API key you provide.

Where data goes

For an AI draft, selected business excerpts, visible conversation content and selected images are sent directly to api.openai.com using your key. Stripe receives only the information needed for the optional one-time license purchase through its hosted Checkout page. The licensing service receives an installation identifier and license status, not your WhatsApp conversations or OpenAI key.

Storage and deletion

Knowledge, notes, follow-ups, settings and the OpenAI key remain in the local Chrome profile until you remove them or uninstall the extension. Remove the saved key and delete knowledge or follow-ups from Settings. OpenAI’s own API data policies apply.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. The OpenAI key is not independently encrypted by WhatsApp Desk; anyone with access to the browser profile or extension developer tools may be able to retrieve it. Do not place API keys or customer messages in support requests.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue in the WhatsApp Desk support tracker ↗. Please describe the request without including secrets or customer content.
