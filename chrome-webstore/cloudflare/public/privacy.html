Privacy policy · effective 25 October 2026

Clear about your data.

Desk AI for WhatsApp Web is an independent open-source project by Kudakwashe Musasiwa. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by Meta or WhatsApp.

What the extension handles

When you enable the relevant features, the extension handles visible WhatsApp Web conversations and contact details, your business documents and notes, loaded images or payment proofs, follow-ups, settings and the OpenAI API key you provide.

Where data goes

For an AI draft, selected business excerpts, visible conversation content and selected images are sent directly to api.openai.com using your key. Optional voice dictation starts after you click the microphone and grant access. The recording is sent to OpenAI for transcription using your key and is not saved by the extension. The returned text is editable in the panel and syncs to the selected WhatsApp composer. Optional call recording starts only after you click Record and choose the active WhatsApp Web tab with Share audio enabled in Chrome's picker. It records tab audio and your microphone for up to 60 minutes and 20 MiB; video is not recorded and audio is not saved by the extension. After Stop, the audio is sent to OpenAI for transcription using your key, together with any business, participant and speaker names you have provided as a transcription hint. For the optional customer-facing call summary, the transcript text, your configured business name and the participant and speaker names are sent to OpenAI. The call summary remains editable and syncs only to the original chat with a compare-and-set check against the current composer. The extension does not send the summary or any message. Optional text polishing sends only the selected operator text to OpenAI, without chat history or business knowledge. The user reviews the text and presses WhatsApp's Send button. AI assistance remains available separately. Stripe receives only the information needed for the optional one-time license purchase through its hosted Checkout page. The licensing service receives an installation identifier and license status, not your WhatsApp conversations or OpenAI key.

Storage and deletion

Knowledge, notes, follow-ups, settings and the OpenAI key remain in the local Chrome profile until you remove them or uninstall the extension. Remove the saved key and delete knowledge or follow-ups from Settings. OpenAI’s own API data policies apply.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. The OpenAI key is not independently encrypted by Desk AI for WhatsApp Web; anyone with access to the browser profile or extension developer tools may be able to retrieve it. Do not place API keys or customer messages in support requests.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue in the Desk AI for WhatsApp Web support tracker ↗. Please describe the request without including secrets or customer content.
